# Полные условия применения

Use when asked Temu 全托管还是半托管, TikTok Shop 怎么入驻, 亚马逊和 Temu 选哪个, 跨境电商平台怎么选, 产品出海要哪些认证, or choose and launch on cross-border marketplaces (Temu, TikTok Shop, Amazon, SHEIN, AliExpress). Produces a platform fit matrix for the seller's product and capacity, the operating model of each shortlisted platform (fully managed, semi-managed or self-operated), the compliance checklist by target market, a 90-day launch plan, and the questions to ask each platform's account manager. For the product listing copy itself, use cross-border-listing.
