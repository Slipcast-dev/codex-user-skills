# Полные условия применения

Write a CV for someone moving into a new field, leading with transferable evidence instead of job titles that do not match. Use when asked to write a CV for a career change, I'm moving from teaching into product, how do I present my experience for a new industry, or my job titles don't fit the jobs I want. Produces a hybrid CV with a targeted summary and a relevant-skills section backed by achievements, a translation table from old-field language to new-field language, and an honest list of gaps with how to close them.
