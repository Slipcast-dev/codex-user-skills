# Полные условия применения

Write a Chinese workplace weekly or monthly report (周报 / 月报) for a manager: results with numbers, progress against goals, risks raised early, next week's plan and the support needed, in the structure Chinese managers expect. Use when asked 帮我写周报, 写月报, 整理本周工作, weekly report in Chinese, or turn my notes into a 周报. Produces the report in Chinese with 本周完成, 关键数据, 问题与风险, 下周计划 and 需要的支持, plus a one-line summary for chat apps.
