# Полные условия применения

Write a Chinese annual self-review (述职报告 / 年终总结) for a performance cycle: results against OKR or KPI with evidence, the two or three things that mattered most, honest lessons, and next year's plan, in the form Chinese employers expect. Use when asked 帮我写年终总结, 写述职报告, 年度绩效自评, 半年总结, or a year-end review in Chinese. Produces the review in Chinese, a one-page summary for the presentation, a data appendix, and likely questions from the reviewer.
