# Полные условия применения

Analyse a site before designing on it — what is physically there, what the law and the neighbours allow, and which constraints will actually shape the building. Use when asked to carry out a site analysis, site appraisal, feasibility study for a plot, or to assess whether a site can take a proposed development. Produces the physical survey summary, the constraints and opportunities map, the statutory and legal position, the developable-capacity estimate, and the risks that need resolving before design starts.
