# Полные условия применения

Compare small loans from microfinance institutions, lending apps, savings groups and moneylenders by what they really cost, including flat versus reducing-balance interest, fees, compulsory savings and group guarantees. Use when someone says 'which microloan is cheaper', 'is this lending app a good deal', 'the interest is only 2% a month, is that good', or 'should I join a group loan'. Produces the true cost of each offer, a side-by-side comparison, the risks in each, and questions to ask before signing.
