# Полные условия применения

Write a US federal resume for a USAJOBS application: the long, detailed format with hours per week, grade-relevant duties and specialised experience written to the announcement's qualifications. Use when asked to write my federal resume, apply on USAJOBS, turn my private-sector resume into a government resume, or qualify for a GS grade. Produces the federal resume, a qualifications crosswalk against the announcement's specialised experience, and a checklist of the details USAJOBS reviewers reject applications for missing.
