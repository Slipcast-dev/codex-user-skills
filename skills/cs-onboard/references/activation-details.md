# Полные условия применения

Founder onboarding interview that captures company context across 7 dimensions. Invoke with /cs-onboard setup for initial interview or /cs-onboard update for quarterly refresh. Generates ~/.claude/company-context.md used by all C-suite advisor skills. Use when setting up the C-suite advisors for the first time, or when company context is missing or more than 90 days old — e.g. after a fundraise or pivot.
