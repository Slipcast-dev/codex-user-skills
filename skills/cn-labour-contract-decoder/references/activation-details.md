# Полные условия применения

Decode a mainland China labour contract (劳动合同) before signing: probation, pay and its structure, working hours, social insurance, non-compete and confidentiality, termination terms and anything that conflicts with the Labour Contract Law. Use when asked 帮我看看劳动合同, 劳动合同有什么坑, 试用期合法吗, 竞业限制条款, or review my Chinese employment contract. Produces a clause-by-clause decode with red, amber and green flags, the statutory limits each clause must respect, the questions to ask HR, and suggested wording for changes. Not legal advice.
