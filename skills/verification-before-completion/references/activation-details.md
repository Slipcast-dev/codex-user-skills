# Полные условия применения

Verify work actually meets its brief BEFORE declaring it done — a structured self-review pass that catches the gaps, unmet requirements, and untested claims that 'looks finished' hides. Use when about to hand over any deliverable (document, code, analysis, plan), when past work kept coming back with 'you missed…', or as the standing final step of any multi-step task. Produces the verified deliverable plus a short verification record: what was checked, what was found and fixed, what remains open.
