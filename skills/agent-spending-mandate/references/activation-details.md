# Полные условия применения

Write a spending mandate that lets an AI agent buy things for you within strict limits: what it may buy, from whom, up to how much per purchase and in total, until when, and what needs your yes. Use when asked to let my agent make purchases, set a budget for an AI shopping agent, allow agentic checkout, limit what my assistant can spend, or prepare for agent payments. Produces the mandate in plain words and as a structured block, the always-ask list and the review and revoke steps.
