# Полные условия применения

Score how well a CV covers a job ad, requirement by requirement, and say honestly what to do about each gap. Use when asked how well do I match this job, score my CV against this job description, what am I missing for this role, or should I apply. Produces a requirement table marked covered, partly covered or missing with the evidence, a fit score with its method shown, and for each gap a truthful framing, a quick way to close it, or advice not to claim it.
