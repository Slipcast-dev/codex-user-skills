# Полные условия применения

Plan a new manager's first 90 days — first-time or new-to-team — as listen/decide/move phases: the 1:1 listening tour with real questions, the early-judgment traps, the quick-wins filter, and the 30/60/90 artifacts. Use when asked I just became a manager what do I do, plan my first 90 days as a manager, first-time manager advice, taking over an existing team, or new manager 30-60-90 plan. Produces the phased plan, the listening-tour question set, the doer-to-enabler mindset shift, the team assessment framework, and the day-one and week-6 artifacts.
