# Полные условия применения

Help a student and family plan university applications after the 高考 (gaokao): use the student's provincial rank rather than raw score, build a 冲 / 稳 / 保 (reach, match, safe) list from official historical admission data, check subject requirements under the new gaokao, and weigh major against university. Use when asked 帮我填高考志愿, 志愿怎么填, 冲稳保怎么排, 这个分数能上什么学校, or plan gaokao applications. Produces a structured application list with the reasoning for each choice, a risk check of the whole list, and the questions to settle as a family. Never guarantees admission.
