# Полные условия применения

Design a proactive agent's morning brief: which connected sources it reads, what earns a line, the fixed section order and length cap, and the wording rules that keep it honest. Produces a brief specification the agent follows every morning: sources, selection rules, an 8-line template with citations, and what to leave out.
