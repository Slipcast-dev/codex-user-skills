# Полные условия применения

Plan a Chinese company's entry into an overseas market (出海): choose the market with evidence, then plan entity, payments, localisation, compliance, channels and team for the first twelve months. Use when asked 出海计划, 我们要进入东南亚 / 中东 / 欧洲 / 美国市场, how should a Chinese company expand overseas, or a go-global plan. Produces a market scorecard, a twelve-month entry plan by workstream, the compliance and data checklist for the chosen market, and the top risks with mitigations. Bilingual output on request.
