# Полные условия применения

Extract every hidden assumption from a plan or document and put a price on each one — what it costs if wrong, what it costs to test. Use when about to commit to anything whose author says 'obviously' or whose spreadsheet has hardcoded cells: the bounty hunt makes the invisible load-bearing beliefs explicit and tells you which three to test this week. Produces the assumption ledger (priced and ranked), the cheapest test for each dangerous one, and the document's honest confidence statement.
