# Полные условия применения

A readiness audit for proactive agents, useful while you are still on the waitlist: what in your memory, recurring prompts, connectors and inbox habits will help or hurt an agent that acts between your messages. Use when asked to prepare for Duet, get ready before my invite lands, audit my setup for a proactive agent, or what should I tidy before connecting my accounts. Produces a scored readiness audit with the five tidy-ups worth doing now, each useful in the ordinary Claude app today even if the invite never comes.
