# Полные условия применения

Use when asked 幼升小怎么准备, 小升初流程, 入学信息采集, 学区房和多校划片, 公民同招, 民办摇号, 随迁子女入学, 居住证入学材料, or plan a child's entry to primary or junior high school in mainland China. Produces a month-by-month timeline for the child's city, the documents to prepare (户籍, 房产 or 租房, 居住证, 社保), the questions to ask the 教育局 or school about 学区, 多校划片 and 派位, a public versus private comparison, and a checklist, with every local rule marked to confirm with the local education bureau.
