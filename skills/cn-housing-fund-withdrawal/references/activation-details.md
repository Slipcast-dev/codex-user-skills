# Полные условия применения

Use when asked 公积金怎么提取, 租房能提公积金吗, 离职后公积金怎么办, 公积金贷款能贷多少, 异地提取公积金, or plan how to use a Chinese housing provident fund (住房公积金). Produces the withdrawal routes the person qualifies for (购房, 还贷, 租房, 离职或离开城市, 退休, 重大疾病 and others), the documents and channels for each, a comparison of withdrawing versus keeping the balance for a 公积金贷款, and the city-specific limits to confirm.
