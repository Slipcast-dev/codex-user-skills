# Полные условия применения

Use when asked 帮我写家长会发言稿, 家长会PPT大纲, 班主任家长会讲话, 期中家长会, 新生家长会, 毕业班家长会, or prepare a parent meeting as a Chinese teacher. Produces a parent-meeting pack in Chinese: an agenda, a timed speech for the 班主任 or subject teacher with class data presented without ranking individual students, a slide outline, specific requests to parents, a one-page handout, answers to likely questions, and a follow-up message for the parents' WeChat group.
