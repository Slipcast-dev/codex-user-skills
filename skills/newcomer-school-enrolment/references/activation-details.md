# Полные условия применения

Get a child into school after moving to a new country: who to contact, the documents schools ask for, what to do when papers are missing, language support, and the right to a place. Use when someone says 'we just moved, how do I enrol my child in school', 'the school wants documents we don't have', 'my child doesn't speak the language yet', or 'which school can my kid go to here'. Produces an enrolment plan in order, a document checklist with fallbacks for missing papers, questions to ask the school, and a first-weeks support plan.
