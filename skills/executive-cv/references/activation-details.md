# Полные условия применения

Write an executive or senior leadership CV and the matching board biography: scope, business results and governance up front, two to three pages, written for search firms and boards. Use when asked to write my executive CV, director or C-suite resume, CV for a headhunter, board bio, or non-executive director application. Produces the executive CV with a scope line per role, a results-led profile, a 150-word board biography in the third person, and a short brief for search consultants.
