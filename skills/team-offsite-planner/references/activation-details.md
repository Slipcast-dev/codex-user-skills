# Полные условия применения

Plan a team offsite that earns its cost — the purpose split (connection vs decisions vs planning, weighted deliberately), an agenda that alternates work and air, the logistics runbook, and the follow-through that makes Monday different. Use when asked to plan a team offsite, away day, team retreat, quarterly offsite, design two days for the team, or make this offsite not a waste. Produces the purpose weighting, a full agenda with session designs, facilitation notes, a logistics checklist, and the follow-through plan.
