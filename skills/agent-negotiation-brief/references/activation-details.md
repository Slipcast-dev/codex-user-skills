# Полные условия применения

Brief an AI agent that will negotiate for you with another party's agent or a person (a refund, a price, a meeting time, a contract term): your goal, your opening, your limits, your walk-away point, what it must never reveal, and when it must come back to you. Use when asked to let my agent negotiate, brief my AI for a negotiation, set limits for an agent bargaining on my behalf, or prepare for agent-to-agent negotiation. Produces the brief, the concession ladder, the never-reveal list and the hand-back triggers.
