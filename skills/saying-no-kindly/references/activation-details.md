# Полные условия применения

Decline requests without damaging relationships or your standing — the fast-clear-warm formula, the alternative-attached no, the no-to-the-boss version (tradeoffs, not refusal), and scripts for the asks that recur. Use when asked how do I say no to this, turn down a request, decline extra work politely, I say yes to everything and drown, push back on my manager, or protect the roadmap from a pet feature. Produces the decline scripts by relationship, the tradeoff framing, and the exact wording tuned to who is asking.
