# Полные условия применения

Explain an official form field by field (tax, benefits, immigration, employment, school, health, vehicle or court forms) from a photo, PDF, pasted text or just its name and number: what it is for, what each field means and what to put, the documents needed, the deadline, and where to send it. Use when someone says 'what is this form', 'help me understand form P45 / W-4 / Form 16', 'what do I write in this box', or 'I got this form in the post'. Produces a one-line purpose, a field-by-field guide, a documents checklist, common mistakes, and what happens next.
