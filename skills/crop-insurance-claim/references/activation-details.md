# Полные условия применения

Make a crop or livestock insurance claim after drought, flood, pests, disease or hail: report it in time, record the loss properly, and follow up or appeal if the payout is too low or refused. Use when someone says 'my crop failed, how do I claim', 'the insurance company rejected my crop claim', 'what proof do I need for a crop loss', or 'how do weather-index insurance payouts work'. Produces a claim timeline, an evidence checklist, a loss record, a notification message, and an appeal plan.
