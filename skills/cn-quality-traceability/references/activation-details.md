# Полные условия применения

Use when asked 质量追溯体系怎么建, 产品追溯方案, 批次追溯, 一物一码, 客户要求可追溯性, 召回演练, 正向追溯和反向追溯, or plan product and batch traceability in a Chinese factory. Produces a traceability plan in Chinese: the trace unit and coding rule, the data to capture at each process step (material lots, work orders, equipment, people, inspection results), forward and backward trace paths, record retention, a mock recall drill with a target time, gaps against the current system, and a phased rollout, with industry rules (food, medical devices, automotive) marked to confirm.
