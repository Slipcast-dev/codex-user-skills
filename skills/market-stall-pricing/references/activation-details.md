# Полные условия применения

Price goods for a market stall, street stall or small shop so every sale covers its costs, and hold your price when customers bargain. Use when someone says 'how much should I sell this for', 'customers always bargain me down', 'am I making any money at the market', or 'what price for my vegetables, crafts or snacks'. Produces a cost-per-item calculation, a floor price, a starting price with room to bargain, bargaining lines that keep the margin, and a quick end-of-day check.
