# Полные условия применения

Apply the CV conventions of the country where the job is: length, photo, personal details, date and name order, spelling, paper size and the documents expected alongside. Use when asked what a CV looks like in Germany, Japan, China, the UK, the US, France, India, the Gulf or Australia, should I put a photo on my CV, convert my US resume to a UK CV, or make a 简历, 履歴書 or Lebenslauf. Produces the country rules as a checklist, the CV converted to them, and the personal details that must be removed or added, with the legal reason where there is one.
