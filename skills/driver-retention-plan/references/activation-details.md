# Полные условия применения

Reduce driver turnover by fixing what actually makes drivers leave — the pay comparison that is real, the home-time reliability, the dispatch relationship, and the first ninety days where most departures are decided. Use when asked to improve driver retention, reduce turnover, understand why drivers are leaving, or design a driver onboarding or referral programme. Produces the turnover analysis by tenure and cause, the true cost of turnover, the exit-driver findings, the prioritised interventions, and the first-90-days plan.
