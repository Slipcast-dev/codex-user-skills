# Полные условия применения

Keep simple daily money records for a small trader, stall or home business with no accounting software: a cash book, stock count and weekly summary that show whether the business makes money and keep business and household money apart. Use when someone says 'I don't know if my shop makes money', 'how do I keep records for my small business', 'the bank wants records for a loan', or 'I mix business and home money'. Produces a one-page daily cash book, a weekly summary, a simple stock count, and three rules to keep the habit.
