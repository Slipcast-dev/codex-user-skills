# Полные условия применения

Use when asked 个体户怎么报税, 小规模纳税人增值税怎么算, 小微企业所得税优惠, 经营所得汇算, 开票超过免税额怎么办, or plan taxes for a sole trader (个体工商户) or small company in China. Produces the taxes that apply (增值税, 附加税费, 个人所得税经营所得 or 企业所得税), the relief that may apply with the announcement to check, a worked estimate from the person's own figures, a filing calendar, and the questions for the local tax office or 12366.
