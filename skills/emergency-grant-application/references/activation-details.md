# Полные условия применения

Apply for emergency money after a disaster, crisis or sudden hardship: government disaster assistance, local hardship and welfare funds, charity crisis grants, and employer or union funds. Use when someone says 'is there emergency money after the flood', 'how do I apply for disaster assistance', 'I need a hardship grant to pay for food and rent', or 'my application for emergency help was refused'. Produces a list of funds to try in order, the documents each usually needs, an application statement, and a follow-up and appeal plan.
