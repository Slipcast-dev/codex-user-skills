# Полные условия применения

Write a one-page CV for management consulting, investment banking or private equity, in the dense format those screeners expect: education first, quantified bullets, leadership and interests that show drive. Use when asked to write my consulting CV, banking resume, PE CV, make my CV for McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Goldman or a boutique bank, or fit my experience on one page for finance. Produces the one-page CV, a bullet-by-bullet strength check, and the three lines a screener will read first.
