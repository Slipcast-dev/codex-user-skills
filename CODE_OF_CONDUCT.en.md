# Code of conduct

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and discuss specific ideas, changes, and verifiable facts.
- Recognize that participants may have different experience, languages, and perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback and respond to feedback respectfully.
- Respect the privacy and intellectual property of participants and third parties.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, sharing someone else's personal information, spam, and targeted personal insults are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainer [@Slipcast-dev](https://github.com/Slipcast-dev) may hide or remove material that violates these rules, restrict participation, or block participants. Decisions should consider context, severity, and repeated behavior. Possible actions include requesting an edit, issuing a warning, temporarily restricting participation, or blocking a participant.

Report violations using GitHub's **Report content** feature on the relevant message or through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact). Do not publish affected people's personal information in an open issue.

The maintainer must also follow these rules. GitHub handles violations of platform rules according to its own terms and policies.
